# Superstore-Business-Analytics

An end-to-end business analytics project using the Superstore dataset to analyze sales, profitability, customer geography, product categories, sub-categories, discounts, and trends over time.

## Project Overview

The objective of this project was to explore business performance through data and identify patterns that could support better commercial decision-making.

The analysis covers:

- Sales performance
- Profitability
- Product and sub-category performance
- Geographic performance
- Monthly trends
- Discount and profit relationships
- Business recommendations

##  Business Questions

- Which product categories generate the highest sales?
- Which categories and sub-categories generate the most profit?
- How do sales and profit change over time?
- Which states and cities contribute most to sales and profitability?
- Which sub-categories show weaker or negative profitability?
- How are discounts distributed across sub-categories?
- Which areas may require further investigation from a business perspective?

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Excel** — Data preparation and analysis
- **SQL / MySQL** — Data querying and database practice
- **Python** — Data analysis and visualization
- **Tableau** — Interactive visualizations and dashboards
- **Alteryx** — Data preparation workflow

## 📊 Analysis & Visualizations

### Sales & Profitability

The analysis compares sales and profit across major product categories and sub-categories to understand which areas contribute most to overall business performance.

### Geographic Analysis

State- and city-level analysis was used to identify geographic differences in sales and profitability.

### Time-Series Analysis

Monthly and yearly trends were analyzed to identify changes in sales, profit, and quantity over time.

### Discount Analysis

Discount levels were compared with sub-category profitability to identify areas where discounting and margins may warrant further investigation.

## Key Insights

- Technology generated the highest sales among the three major product categories.
- Technology also generated the highest category-level profit.
- Furniture generated lower profit than Technology and Office Supplies.
- Profitability varies substantially across sub-categories, with some sub-categories showing negative profit.
- Sales and profitability vary considerably across geographic locations.
- Some sub-categories combine relatively high discount levels with weaker profitability, creating an area for further investigation.

## Business Recommendations

- Monitor sub-categories with negative or weak profitability.
- Investigate the relationship between discounting and profit margins before increasing discount levels.
- Review geographic differences in sales and profitability to identify areas requiring additional attention.
- Monitor high-performing categories and sub-categories to understand the factors contributing to their performance.
- Combine sales, profit, discount, and geographic analysis when making commercial decisions rather than relying on sales volume alone.
